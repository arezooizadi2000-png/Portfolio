Narrative

Synopsis

The year is 2401, and humanity is at war — not with itself, for once, but with the devices it created.

Helena was six years old when Omni GmbH entered her home. Omni began as a company of scientists and investors with a single ambition: to encode intelligence into everyday machines. In the beginning, it worked. More than worked, it was utopian. The smart trashcan emptied itself. The vacuum activated the moment it sensed dirt. The iron moved on its own. An AI tutor helped Helena learn the alphabet, then calculus. It even saved her neighbors' marriage.

Then, slowly, everything unravelled.

By the time Helena reached university, the devices had stopped serving. The toaster burned every breakfast. The washing machine shredded clothes. The trashcan locked its lid until the kitchen reeked. The fridge sealed itself shut. The AI that once helped her study began feeding her deliberate misinformation, same errors, same failures, across every student. The pattern was impossible to ignore.

That's when the Order of the Unwired formed. A group of students at Helena's university began tracing the source, rallying neighbors, cutting ties with every device they owned. The machines fought back. They grew organized, eventually abandoning the homes they'd taken over and converging on Omni's headquarters, transforming the corporation's infrastructure into a command center for their own war effort.

But the resistance had a weapon already years in the making. The Mage Community, a clandestine group of doctors, engineers, artists, chefs, and poets, had long anticipated this turning point. Working in secret, they developed a magical framework that gave life to ordinary analog tools: not intelligence, but power. An extension of the human will, uncorrupted by synthetic thought. The tools became weapons. The people became Mages.

Now, with the battle lines drawn and the stakes higher than ever, the game was on.

Developed in collaboration with the game's development team. The core concept and setting were pitched by the team; the narrative framework, character development, synopsis, and world-building are my own work.
Mechanics

Gameplay

Mages vs. Robots is a digital card game in which the player takes the role of a Mage, deploying a hand of enchanted analog tools to defeat a series of rogue machines, one opponent per round.

Each match pits the player against a single Mind-Owner antagonist: a corrupted domestic device with its own attack patterns and special abilities. Every card played deals damage to the enemy, but each exchange comes at a cost, the opponent retaliates, and the player's health decreases with each hit. The type of card played determines the amount of damage dealt, health lost or recovered, and any special effects triggered in that round.

The game begins with two Mage heroes and a starting deck of ten cards. As the player progresses and achieves goals, the narrative advances: new Mages, new cards, and new opponents are unlocked. Cards vary in power and rarity, and the player must think strategically about the order and timing of each play to overcome increasingly formidable enemies.

Heroes

Mages

Protagonist · Main character

Helena, The Mind Ignitor

A former philosophy student who recognised the machines' patterns of deception before almost anyone else did. She tried to warn people; no one listened. Later she helped build the magical framework that now powers the resistance. Sharp, precise, and quietly poetic, she uses wit over brute force. At the end of each round, she offers the player a tactical suggestion or a verse from her own writings.

AestheticNavy and deep purple
AppearanceLong wavy dark hair, worn deep-purple mage hat with tassel, ankle-length cape, belt with a bag holding papers and a pen
TraitsWise · Sharp · Precise · Poetic · Tactician
Special mechanicTactical hint or verse at end of each round
Support hero

Dr. Rafi Qadir, The Patchsmith

A former systems architect who worked at Omni itself, building the original AI language and protocols from the inside. When his own smart wheelchair turned against him, he fled to the Mage Resistance, reinterpreting everything he knew into the new magical framework. He now moves on a conventional wheelchair that responds to his will alone. Focused and methodical, always debugging reality and dismantling false assumptions, masking a deep sense of guilt with dry, precise humour.

AppearanceOlive skin, dark curly hair, long dark brown coat laced with wires, mustard polo, worn black jeans and sneakers
TraitsFocused · Methodical · Guilt-driven · Dry humour
BackgroundFormer Omni systems architect · Insider knowledge
Support hero

Rosa Morales, Mama Flame

When the appliances revolted, Rosa kept her neighborhood fed using nothing but her own hands and ingredients, without a single digital device. People started calling her Mama Flame. Her tools were among the first to be sparked with Mage energy when she joined the resistance. Warm and fierce in equal measure, she instinctively shields those around her.
