Narrative

Synopsis

Dead-Eye Jane was once one of the League of Shooters' most promising rising talents — until a single night of calculated violence ended her brother Jacob's life and cost her an eye.

It began with a rigged match. Jake and his associate Alan, threatened by Jacob's growing reputation in the League, secretly swapped live rounds for blanks before the game. Jacob was shot dead. Jane — targeted to eliminate the only witness — took a bullet to her right eye instead. The chaos they engineered didn't leave them untouched: Alan was killed by a stray shot, and Jake suffered a fall that shattered his leg. It should have finished him. Instead, Jake leveraged the disorder he created to seize the League's presidency, burying the truth beneath layers of political power and rigged competition.

Years later, Jane walks back into the League's halls. She goes by Dead-Eye Jane now. She has spent every day of her recovery training — harder, sharper, and with a clarity of purpose that leaves no room for doubt. Jacob's weapons are at her side. The whispers start the moment she signs her name at the registration desk: she's back. Across the room, Jake stands at the centre of a group of men, cane in hand and a scornful smile on his face. "Well, if it ain't Dead-Eye Jane. Come to lose again?"

Jane doesn't answer. She lets her performance speak — cutting through the early levels with the precision of someone who has been preparing for exactly this. But the opposition is more than competitive. An old friend, Emily, confirms what Jane already suspected: Jake has systematically rigged the League's structure to protect his inner circle and push out anyone who poses a genuine threat. Jane's return isn't just personal — it's a disruption to an entire corrupt ecosystem.

As the levels grow harder, so does the interference. Voice guides are manipulated, directions are falsified, and Jake's presence is felt even when he isn't in the room. His voice cuts in at moments of pressure: "You think I regret what I did? Jacob was weak. And you? Stubborn and naive." When Jane is briefly injured by what should have been a controlled drill, she confronts Jake directly — forcing into the open his role in Alan's death, Jacob's murder, and the institution he has hollowed out for his own gain. The confrontation ends with one demand: a one-on-one match. Jake agrees, certain he has already won.

He hasn't. In their final battle, Jane takes a graze from a live round, recognises the trick immediately, and turns his overconfidence against him. She shoots him in both legs. He falls. Standing over him, she delivers her verdict: "Get your dirty claws out of this place, Jake. It was never yours to begin with." One final strike closes the chapter.

With Jake removed, the League begins to breathe again. Jane doesn't celebrate. She has brought justice to Jacob's memory — and that, for now, is enough.

Developed as part of a Game Narrative Design certificate on Coursera. The original story premise was provided by the course instructor; the narrative rewrite, character voice, and structural development are my own work.
Cast

Characters

Protagonist

Dead-Eye Jane

Black hair in a tight ponytail. A black eye-patch over her right eye — the permanent mark of the night that changed everything. Her jumpsuit cycles through dark greens and navy blues, short-sleeved to show the tattoos beneath: the most prominent, a gun with drops of blood falling from the barrel. Jacob's weapon rides on her hip. It never leaves.

To those she trusts, Jane is fiercely loyal and genuinely warm. But when it comes to Jake, that clarity can fracture. Her rage is a resource she hasn't fully learned to manage. What steadies her — always — is the memory of Jacob's smile. Soft and unhurried, it's the image she carries into every confrontation.

Resilient Determined Agile Fiercely loyal Rage-driven Quietly tender
Weapon Jacob's — always
Defining feature Black eye-patch, right eye
Arc Grief → justice → release
Antagonist

Jake

Tall and broad-shouldered, he dresses with deliberate casualness: jeans, t-shirt sleeves rolled to the elbow. He always carries a pocket knife and never goes anywhere without his cane — the legacy of the fall that should have ended his career. He could manage without it. He chooses not to. The sound of it striking the floor down a corridor is enough to change the atmosphere of a room before he even enters it.

He has no regret over Jacob's death. None over Alan's. The League is his, built on manipulation and buried evidence — and he intends to keep it that way. He has never met an opponent who didn't break. He doesn't know what to do with one who won't.

Ruthless Power-hungry Strategically cruel Intimidating Unrepentant
Signature Cane, pocket knife
Position League President
Weakness Overconfidence
World-building

Setting

The Shooting Field Dynamic Environment

The Shooting Field is a concrete room that grows with the player — expanding in both size and psychological pressure as the levels advance. It is never just a backdrop. It is an active participant in the difficulty, and at its most hostile, a physical reflection of the rigged and manipulated world Jane is fighting to dismantle.

In the early levels, the field is spare and contained: concrete walls, static targets, windows that show nothing beyond them — just more grey, as though the world outside doesn't exist. The lighting shifts between cold green and flat grey, never settling. A wall fan hums. Speakers occupy each corner, silent for now.

As the levels climb, the environment begins to push back. Targets start moving. The speakers come alive — and in the harder levels, the guidance they offer turns unreliable, issuing misdirection dressed as instruction. The lighting shifts mid-level at the worst possible moments, forcing a reset of focus precisely when the player can least afford it.

Early levels

Contained room. Static targets. Cold green and grey lighting. Silent speakers.

Mid levels

Moving targets. Voice guides activate. Lighting begins to shift mid-round.

Hard levels

Voices turn deceptive. Live opponents. Faulty gear. Maze arena finale.

Gameplay & level design

Acts & Missions

Three acts. Twelve core missions. Five optional. Swipe through each act below — optional missions are marked in gold.

Act I

The Reawakening

Jane returns to the League. The world meets her with taunts and whispers. The player learns the mechanics while the story sets its stakes.

1
Mission 01

Return & Reload

Jane signs up at the League. Jake's contempt arrives immediately. A flashback to Jacob's death sets the emotional stakes before the player fires a single shot. The core mechanics are introduced through tutorial play.

Tutorial · Cutscene · Flashback
2
Mission 02

League Trials I

A standard timed challenge that teaches the rhythm of competitive play and what it means to perform under the League's rules. The field is fair — for now.

Timing challenge · Standard rules

Optional Bonus

Training Day

An extended return to fundamentals for players who want deeper mechanical grounding. No narrative stakes — just time, precision, and repetition.

Extended tutorial
3
Mission 03

Bonds

A mission featuring differently shaped targets, followed by a cutscene: Jane reconnects with Emily, and together they piece together the full shape of Jake's betrayal and what it cost Jacob.

Varied targets · Story cutscene
4
Mission 04

Sabotage

Jane discovers a hidden gear room Jake has been concealing from the League. What she finds there hardens her resolve. She isn't here to compete. She's here to win.

Discovery · Gear unlock
★
Optional Bonus

Memorial

Jane visits Jacob's grave and makes her promise out loud for the first time. The only mission set outdoors — a quiet, open field instead of concrete walls.

Outdoor setting · Emotional beat
Act II

Eye On The Enemy

The League shows its teeth. Jake's interference moves from passive obstruction to active sabotage — and Jane begins to fight a system designed to reject her.

5
Mission 05

League Trials II

An intentionally rigged match. Jane's gear is faulty — some targets don't register, others score zero. The arena voices eventually reveal the tampering, halfway through, when it's already done its damage.

Faulty gear · Revelatory voice cues
6
Mission 06

Unruly

Jake escalates. Jane now faces two live opponents alongside the moving targets. A new mechanic is introduced: the ability to slow time, giving the player a critical edge in moments of chaos.

New mechanic: slow-time · Live opponents
★
Optional Bonus

The Log

Jane finds Alan's old journal. In it, the setup of Jacob's death is documented in Alan's own hand — the clearest evidence yet. It fuels her into the final stretch of the act.

Lore discovery · Motivation boost
7
Mission 07

Network Down

Jane and Emily hack Jake's surveillance system, cutting off his ability to interfere in real time. Jane completes the level cleanly, and walks away with upgraded gear.

Interference-free · Gear upgrade
8
Mission 08

Fire on the Front

A public duel against Jake's most trusted lieutenant, who has publicly bet against Jane. Victory here unravels the lies Jake has told his inner circle. The cracks begin to show.

Public duel · Alliance fracture
Act III

One Last Round

Jane brings everything into the open and forces the confrontation the whole game has been building toward. The ending is not fixed — the player decides Jake's fate.

9
Mission 09

League Trials III

Jake's most elaborate sabotage yet: rigged opponents, compromised gear, corrupted directions. The player must use every tool and technique accumulated across both previous acts to survive it.

All mechanics combined · Maximum difficulty
10
Mission 10

Uncovered

Jane goes public. Jake's lies are leaked to the entire League, and the fallout forces both parties into agreement: a final duel, one-on-one, in a maze arena. The terms are set.

Story revelation · Final terms set
★
Optional Bonus

Gunsmith

A trial run through the maze, and a chance to help Emily build a prototype weapon before the final match. Completing this gives Jane a meaningful edge going into the last level.

Maze preview · Prototype weapon
★
Optional Bonus

Reflection

A final flashback played as Jacob. A rare shift in perspective that reframes everything. Completing it unlocks a letter Jacob wrote to Jane — never sent, now finally read.

Play as Jacob · Unlockable letter
11
Mission 11

Final Shot

Jane enters the maze. Twists, traps, and confrontations accumulate toward the moment the game has been building to since its opening cutscene. Everything she has learned, here, now.

Maze arena · Climactic duel
12
Mission 12

The Clearing

The aftermath. A dialogue-heavy final level in which Jake's defeat is processed by everyone who witnessed it. In the game's closing moments, the player makes one last choice: exile or death. Jane's revenge is complete either way.

Branching ending · Player choice
